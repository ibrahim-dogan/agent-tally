Know when your agents need you.

AgentTally turns a ~$15 ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" into a desk monitor for your AI coding sessions. Every Claude Code session shows up live: working, waiting for your approval, or done. USB only, no Wi-Fi, nothing leaves your computer.

01Flash the firmware

Plug the board in with a USB data cable, pick your model and press Install. Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop. Only the 2.8" dual-USB board has been tested on real hardware; the other images build but are untested, and reports are welcome.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. The installer needs a secure (https) page. Firmware v0.1.0 The bridge is running: it lets go of the USB port while you install and reconnects afterwards.
Port not showing up? Most boards use a CH340 USB-serial chip: macOS and Linux include a driver, Windows may need the WCH CH340 driver. On dual-USB boards use a USB-A to USB-C cable: the board's USB-C port lacks the resistors that C-to-C cables need.

02Install the bridge

A small Python script (Python 3.8+, standard library only) receives Claude Code hook events and mirrors your sessions to the display over the same USB cable. It also autostarts at login. Tested on macOS; Linux is untested and there is no installer for Windows.

# macOS / Linux curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ibrahim-dogan/agent-tally/main/host/install.sh | sh

Then restart your Claude Code sessions and check with agenttally status. Remove the hooks and the service with agenttally uninstall. If ~/.local/bin is not on your PATH, run python3 ~/.agent-tally/agenttally.py instead of agenttally.

  1. Copies agenttally.py to ~/.agent-tally/ and links it as ~/.local/bin/agenttally
  2. Adds hook entries to ~/.claude/settings.json (a backup is saved next to it)
  3. Starts the bridge with launchd (macOS) or a systemd user unit (Linux)
  4. Other agents can report too: agenttally send --source codex --id run1 --state done
What you get on the display. When a session starts waiting for you or finishes, the edge of the screen pulses red or green three times and its card glows. Tap a session for details. At night (22:00 to 09:00, adjustable below) the screen and LED switch off; a touch shows the screen for 30 seconds.
macOS extras. The Focus button in a session's detail view brings its terminal tab to the front (exact tab in iTerm2 and Terminal, other apps are just brought forward). Push the mouse off the bottom-left edge of your main screen and it moves onto the display; switch that off with agenttally pointer off. More in the README.

04Print the stand

A portrait stand that leans the screen back 15°, hides the USB plug and cable inside the foot and lets the cable out at the back. Four parts, no supports. Parametric OpenSCAD source included (tilt, plug room, fit, label). Tested with the 2.8" board. The stand comes with the name in a second colour, engraved, or with no lettering. A light pipe that carries the rear status LED to the bezel is available as an experimental variant; its light is weak, so the red or green frame on the screen is the alert to rely on.

  1. Print front face down, back outside down, one stand upright with ≥30% infill, and the plate.
  2. Put the board in the front bezel, close it with the back cover and 4× M3×14 countersunk screws.
  3. Feed the cable up through the foot, plug it in and seat the body in the socket.
  4. Press the cable into the tunnel and snap the plate on. Add rubber feet.
front.stl 2.8" back.stl 2.8" plate.stl 2.8" stand.stl engraved name stand_text.stl letters, 2nd colour stand-plain.stl no lettering

The experimental light pipe parts, the .scad source and the 3.5" files are in the repository. The 3.5" stand uses estimated dimensions and is untested.
